About Kai

Laughter is medicine

Presenter Kai is a Kenyan presenter, street entertainer and music artist. His page name, Kicheko Dawa, is Swahili for “laughter is medicine”, and it explains everything he does: real people, honest answers and a lot of laughter.

  • Kicheko DawaKai started out hosting on Nakuru-based mainstream media.
  • Going independentHe stepped away to build his own platform on social media.
  • The street is the studioStreet quizzes and exclusive interviews on YouTube, Facebook and TikTok.
  • MusicHis song “Mipango yao imefeli” passed half a million views.

Background from a public interview with Nakuru Times (2023).
